# SIH Demo Flow (3–4 minutes)

1. Log in with `demo@safespace.app` / `demo123`.
2. Show personalised dashboard and theme/name initial.
3. Complete a check-in.
4. Open Safe Space 360 / Insights and explain Emotional Weather + reasons.
5. Update Justice Journey to Hearing.
6. Show support signal changes and counsellor request.
7. Open counsellor dashboard on laptop and record follow-up.
8. Show Recovery Map / Healing Garden to end on progress, not only risk.
9. Briefly show Privacy, Discreet Mode, permissions and offline/IVRS architecture.
