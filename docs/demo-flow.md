# Hackathon Demo Flow

Use the synthetic demo only. Do not mix presentation records with real participant data.

## Mobile story

1. Launch Safe Space and show the animated brand opening: “Your wellbeing. Your space.”
2. Use demo login: demo@safespace.app / demo123.
3. Show the contextual Home greeting and the five-tab navigation.
4. Tap Check-in and save a mood.
5. Choose “Add details” and complete sleep, stress, energy, safety and connection.
6. Open Insights / Safe Space 360. Explain that recent data is compared with the user's own baseline.
7. Point to the reasons: lower mood, less sleep and higher stress. Emphasize that this is not diagnosis.
8. Open Journal, type a real reflection and save it. Show “Saved privately.”
9. Open Settings > Privacy & Security. Show that journal intelligence and support sharing are opt-in.
10. Open Calm and run the breathing experience.
11. Open Companion and show supportive, non-therapist language.
12. Request support.

## Staff story

1. Start the demo backend.
2. Open admin-dashboard/index.html.
3. Sign in with admin@safespace.app / demo123.
4. Show overview, explainable alert, support request and audit trail.
5. Explicitly point out that journal text is absent from the staff API/dashboard.

## Repeatable judge reset

Settings > Presentation Demo > Reset scenario seeds the local five-day synthetic pattern:
Good → Okay → Low → Low → Overwhelmed, with sleep decreasing and stress increasing.

The backend fixture contains the corresponding synthetic story. It is intentionally separate from the Android local demo records.
